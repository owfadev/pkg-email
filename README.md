# pkg-email
## Why you'd want this

Your mailbox becomes a triaged knowledge surface instead of a guilt pile: thread digests, people pages and subscription governance, all local.

- The agent reads so you don't drown — per-thread topic conclusions accumulate append-only, people get one relationship page across accounts
- Read-only by design (PEEK): the CLI never marks, moves or deletes; sending always needs your explicit request
- Credentials stay in your local home; deprecated sources are kept, never silently erased
